Privacy policy

Backup Vault is a personal backup tool used only by its owner.

Data the app uses

The app reads files on the owner's own laptop and phone, encrypts them on the device, and uploads the encrypted copies to the owner's own Google Drive and the owner's private Telegram channel.

Google Drive access

The app asks only for the "drive.file" permission, so it can see and change only the files it creates. It cannot read any other file in the Drive.

Sharing

No data is sent to the app's developer or to any third party. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising. Backups are encrypted before they leave the device and can be opened only with the owner's password.

Deleting data

The owner can delete the backups at any time by removing the "Backups" folder from Google Drive, and can remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
