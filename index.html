Backup Vault

Backup Vault is a personal backup tool. It makes encrypted, incremental backups of one laptop and one phone and stores them in the owner's own Google Drive.

It is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Google Drive access is limited to the files the app itself creates (the "drive.file" permission). The app cannot see or change any other file in the Drive.
